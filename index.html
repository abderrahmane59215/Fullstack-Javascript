Omelette Recipe

An easy and quick dish,perfect for any meal.

Preparation Time

  • Total Time: 10 minutes
  • Preparation Time: 3 minutes
  • Cooking Time: 7 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • Salt to taste
  • Black pepper to taste
  • 1 tablespoon milk (optional)

Instructions

  1. Beat the eggs in a bowl with salt, pepper, and milk.
  2. Heat the butter in a non-stick pan over medium heat.
  3. Pour the egg mixture into the pan.
  4. Cook until the eggs begin to set.
  5. Fold the omelette in half.
  6. Cook for another minute and serve hot.
